Tuscan Sun Salad

Fresh garden greens, charred artisan bread, and sun-ripened tomatoes.

Instructions

Cooking Mode Active
1

Wash the baby spinach and cherry tomatoes thoroughly. Pat dry with a kitchen towel to ensure crispness.

2

Toast the sourdough slices until golden brown. Once toasted, rub a clove of garlic on one side for extra aroma.

3

Assemble by tossing spinach with tomatoes, feta, and olive oil. Drizzle with balsamic glaze and serve immediately.
